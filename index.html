About Us

A Legacy of Learning & Growth

Founded in 1995, The Kalyan School has been a beacon of progressive education in New Delhi. We believe every child is unique and deserves an environment that challenges, inspires, and supports their individual journey.

Our CBSE-affiliated curriculum blends academic rigor with co-curricular excellence, preparing students not just for exams, but for life.
